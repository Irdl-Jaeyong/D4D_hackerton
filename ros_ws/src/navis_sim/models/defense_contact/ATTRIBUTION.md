Original procedural 12 m patrol/RHIB visualization meshes generated by scripts/build_threat_vessels.py for this project. No third-party vessel mesh was imported. Units: metres. These visual meshes are not a validated vessel dynamics model. Wake visuals use the separately attributed existing ocean mesh.

The fixed-wing airframe and rounded multirotor shell are also original procedural meshes generated by the same script. No external aircraft asset was imported.
